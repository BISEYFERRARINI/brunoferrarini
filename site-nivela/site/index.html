Questions

Straight answers

Still unsure? Open the demo and click around. It's the fastest way to see if Nivela fits your company.

Do I pay per user?

No. Every plan already includes office and field users. You only pay a small fee for each user above your plan's limit.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Install it on iPhone, Android, tablet or computer in seconds, straight from the browser. No app store needed.

Can I try it first?

Yes. You get 14 days free, no credit card, with a demo company already loaded so you can test proposals, costs and the time clock right away.

My crew speaks Portuguese and my clients speak English. Does that work?

That's exactly what Nivela was built for. Each person chooses their language, and proposals and reports go out in the language of the client who receives them.

Will my employees see prices and profit?

No. Field users see the job, tasks, plans, crew updates and the time clock. Dollar amounts, costs and profit stay with the owner and the people you allow.

I run two companies. Do I need two accounts?

No. The Business plan holds up to 3 companies in one account, each with its own logo, colors and proposals. You switch between them with one tap.

What counts toward the AI limit?

Each proposal created by voice and each receipt read by AI counts once. If you reach your plan's limit, you can still enter things by hand or move up a plan.

What happens when the trial ends?

You choose a plan to keep going. Since we never asked for a card, nothing is charged automatically.
