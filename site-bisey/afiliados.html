Affiliate disclosure

Last updated: October 27, 2026

The short version: some links to stores on getbisey.com and inside the Bisey app may be affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, Bisey may earn a commission from the store. You pay the same price, and the commission never changes what we show you.

Where these links appear

  • On our public Material lists & where to buy page.
  • Inside the Bisey app, on the “View at” store links of a job’s material list.
  • Occasionally in help articles that point to a product or store.

We put a short notice next to these links and link back to this page.

What it doesn’t change

  • Your price. An affiliate link never adds anything to what you pay at the store.
  • The prices Bisey shows. We don’t display store prices. Prices inside Bisey come from your own price list and the deals you save.
  • Store order. Stores are listed by your preferences and the stores typical of each trade, not by commission. If that ever changes, we will say so on this page and next to the links.

No paid placement

No store or brand pays to appear in our sample lists, buying tips or material lists. Products and brands we name are common examples contractors use, not endorsements, and you can buy the same items anywhere you like.

Current status

We are applying to affiliate programs run by retailers and affiliate networks (such as Impact, CJ, Awin and Amazon Associates). As of October 27, 2026, Bisey has no active affiliate partners and no link on this site earns a commission. When a program approves us, we will list it below with the date and add any wording that program requires.

Active partners: none yet.

Cookies on store sites

When you click a store link you leave Bisey. The store, and for affiliate links the affiliate network, may set cookies on their own sites to credit the referral. Their privacy policies apply there. Networks usually report order totals and commissions to us, not your name or payment details. See our Privacy Policy.

Questions

Write to hello@getbisey.com.
