Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

This policy explains what information [Company legal name] ("we") collects when you use Bisey, how we use it and the choices you have.

1. What we collect

  • Account data: name, email, phone, company name, logo and language.
  • Business data you enter: jobs, clients, proposals, invoices, expenses, receipts, prices, tasks, files and photos.
  • Time clock data: clock-in and clock-out times, selfie photos and GPS location at the moment of each punch.
  • Voice and images for AI: audio recorded for voice proposals and photos of receipts, processed to produce text and data.
  • Technical data: device, browser, IP address and basic usage logs, to keep the service secure and working.

2. How we use it

To provide the service, process AI features, send emails you request (such as proposals, daily reports and weekly timesheets), give support, keep accounts secure and improve Bisey. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Service providers

We use these providers. They may process data only on our behalf and only for the purpose listed:

  • Netlify (United States): hosting of the website and the app, and the server functions of the support chat.
  • Supabase (United States): database, login and file storage, once accounts are live. While the app runs in demo mode, data stays only on your device.
  • Anthropic (United States): AI that writes proposals from your voice or text, reads receipts and powers the Bisey Assist support chat.
  • OpenAI (United States): backup AI provider, used only if the main provider (Anthropic) fails.
  • Google Workspace (United States): support email (hello@getbisey.com).
  • Stripe (United States): subscription payments, once paid plans are live. We never see or store your full card number.

AI and the support chat. Only what is needed for each request is sent to the AI providers (for example, the text of a proposal, the photo of a receipt or your question in the chat). Under these providers' API terms, the data sent through their APIs is not used to train their models. Support chat conversations (on the website, in the app and by email) are stored for up to 365 days and then deleted, so we can answer you and improve the answers.

4. Location and selfies

Location is collected only when a user clocks in or out, not continuously. Selfies are used to confirm who punched in. The account owner decides who can see these records.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep data while the account is active and for [period] after cancellation, unless the law requires otherwise. You can ask us to export or delete your data at any time. Support chat conversations are deleted after 365 days (see section 3).

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, California under the CCPA/CPRA, or Brazil under the LGPD), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data and to object to certain uses. Contact us to exercise these rights.

7. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and [other measures]. No system is 100% secure; we will notify you of incidents as required by law.

8. Children

Bisey is a business tool and is not intended for people under 18.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: hello@getbisey.com [confirm address].
